# Native activity device and zone evidence

The native history read exposes the same five FIT device and zone families
and the same approved fields. PostgreSQL selects those fields before
transferring the rows. The view keeps each activity's records separate,
validates the saved parser generation and reports current, stale and
unavailable evidence. It reports the settings and zones recorded in the activity.

Fourteen responses matched pinned Python, including ordering, activity and
record limits, Unicode fields and the first-row and later-row byte budgets.
An unreturned 16 MiB private serial field remained in PostgreSQL. Another
check verified the replay gate. The Linux ARM64 fixture peaked at
7,766,016 bytes in a 32 MiB container with no swap.
